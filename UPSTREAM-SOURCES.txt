The official Scratch source archives are linked at /source/ on the same host. Exact revisions, source manifests, checksum-pinned URLs, notices and restoration instructions are included under public/source in this ZIP. Run node scripts/restore-source-packets.mjs to fetch and verify all archive packets. The laptop handoff already contains them. See docs/source-distribution.md. Package lockfiles pin the complete dependency tree. The unmodified ML weights and Scratch media are distributed separately at /models/ and /scratch-media/ with provenance/checksums. The scripts prepare-ml-models.mjs and package-scratch-media.mjs verify or restore them. No user projects, credentials or installed dependencies are included.
